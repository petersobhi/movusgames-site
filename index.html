How it works

Prop the phone up. Stand back. Move.

There is nothing to pair, nothing to charge and nothing to hold. The whole set-up is where you put the phone and how much room you leave yourself.

01

Stand it at waist height

Leaning against a wall, on a shelf, propped on a table edge. Flat on the floor is the one place it cannot see you properly.

02

Step back two or three metres

Calibration draws a live outline of you and turns green the moment your whole body is in frame. That is the only setting there is.

03

Play with your whole body

Every game reads a real movement — a step across, a crouch, a jump, a lean, a punch — rather than a gesture you have to learn.

Privacy

The camera never leaves your phone.

A game that watches you has to be worth trusting. Every frame Movus reads is processed on the device and thrown away in the same breath — there is no upload, no account, and no copy of you anywhere.

Nothing is recorded No video, no photos, no still frames. Nothing is written to disk and nothing is sent anywhere.
No account, no name Movus never asks who you are. There is nothing to sign in to and nothing to sign out of.
Analytics you can switch off Anonymous aggregates only — which game, how long, how many movements — and one switch in Settings that stops them.

It counts

A real workout, measured honestly.

Movus counts calories, reps and streaks, and writes your sessions to Apple Health if you let it.

  • Energy is estimated from your own body — the Mifflin‑St Jeor equation against each activity's measured intensity — rather than from a flat table.
  • It reports active energy, the part above resting metabolism, which is what Apple Health means by the number.
  • Height, weight, age and sex come from Health when you allow it, and fall back to population medians when you do not.
  • Every session is logged with the movements it actually read, so a round you barely moved in does not count as one you did.

What you need

An iPhone and a bit of floor.

  • An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
  • Somewhere to prop it at about waist height or above.
  • Two to three metres of space to stand back into.
  • Room to swing your arms without hitting a lamp.
