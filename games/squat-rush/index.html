Squat Rush

Three pitching machines in a park take aim at you, and the only way out of the way is down. Squat under every ball, come back up, and keep your three hearts as the volleys speed up.

Intensity
8.0 MET
Active energy
≈73 kcal / 10 min*
Controller
Your body

How to play

The moves it reads.

Squat

Bend your knees and get low until the ball passes. Hold it long enough to clear it.

Stand

Come back up between balls — every one is a full repetition.

Watch the lamps

A machine lights up before it fires, so you know which one is next.

The workout

What Squat Rush works.

Quads, glutes and hamstrings with every squat, and your heart rate from the pace. It is a bodyweight squat workout in disguise.

*For a 35-year-old, 170 cm, 70 kg adult playing continuously. The app uses your own height, weight and age. How it is calculated.

Tips

  • Push your hips back as you squat, like sitting into a chair.
  • Keep your chest up so the camera reads your height clearly.
  • Two machines can fire at once — the lamps tell you.
  • Squat for every ball, even the ones you think will miss.

Questions

Squat Rush FAQ

How many squats is a game?

It depends how long you last — the app counts every one and shows the total at the end.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Yes. Squat as deep as is comfortable; the game only needs your head to get under the ball.

How many calories does it burn?

About 73 kcal in ten minutes for a 70 kg adult — repeated bodyweight squats add up fast.
