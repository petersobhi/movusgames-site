Six games, one controller.

Each Movus game asks your body for something different — a step, a squat, a punch, a stroke. Pick by what you want to work, or just by what looks fun.

Move Rush

An endless runner through a dusk city. Step left and right to change lane, jump the barriers, squat under the gantries.

6.5 MET · ≈58 kcal per 10 min

Penalty Hero

Stand in goal and save penalties with your own hands. Reach high, reach wide, get low — every save is a real dive.

4.5 MET · ≈37 kcal per 10 min

Swim

Race three lanes to the wall with a real front-crawl stroke. Reach over, pull through, and keep the rhythm.

5.0 MET · ≈42 kcal per 10 min

Slash

Your hands are blades. Swipe through fruit thrown over a rooftop at sunset — and leave the bombs alone.

4.5 MET · ≈37 kcal per 10 min

Squat Rush

Three machines fire balls at your head. Squat under every one. A leg workout that does not feel like one.

6.0 MET · ≈52 kcal per 10 min

Boxing

First-person boxing. Throw real punches, slip his jab, and land the counter. A new opponent every fight.

5.5 MET · ≈47 kcal per 10 min
