Is camera-based fitness private?

Movus guides · Updated 2026-10-09

A game that watches you has to be worth trusting. Here is what to ask of any camera fitness app — and the answers for Movus.

The questions to ask

  • Is the video processed on the phone or sent to a server?
  • Is anything recorded or saved?
  • Does it need an account?
  • What data does it collect, and can you turn it off?

The answers for Movus

  • On the phone. Every frame is read by Apple's on-device body tracking and discarded. There is no server that receives video.
  • Nothing recorded. No video, no photos, no still frames are written or sent.
  • No account. Movus never asks for a name or an email.
  • Anonymous usage, switchable. Which game, how long and how many movements — aggregates only, never images or body data — with one switch in Settings to stop it.

The camera light

When a game is running, iOS shows its camera indicator. Movus closes the camera whenever you leave a game, so the indicator goes off on the home screen.

The full details are in the privacy policy.
