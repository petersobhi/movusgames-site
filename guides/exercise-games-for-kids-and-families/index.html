Active games for kids and families at home

Movus guides · Updated 2026-11-05

When it is raining, dark early, or just too cold to go out, a game that needs you to jump, duck and dodge gets the whole house moving — and there is no controller to share.

Why body games suit families

  • No controller to learn. Children already know how to jump and squat. Each game shows a short animation of the move it wants.
  • Easy turn-taking. Rounds last a minute or two, so everyone gets a go.
  • Visible scores. Personal bests make it a friendly competition.

Good first games

  • Squat Rush — one simple move, instantly funny.
  • Slash — just your hands; works for small children and seated players.
  • Move Rush — the classic endless runner, with real jumping.

Safety first

  • Clear the space and keep younger children and pets out of the play area.
  • Play on a non-slip floor in trainers or bare feet, not socks.
  • Stand the phone where it cannot be knocked over.
