No-equipment cardio at home that doesn't feel like cardio

Movus guides · Updated 2026-10-09

Cardio does not need a treadmill. Jumps, squats, side-steps and punches, done continuously, raise your heart rate as well as most machines — the hard part is keeping going. Games solve that part.

The movements that do the work

  • Jumps — the quickest way to raise your heart rate.
  • Squats — big muscles, big demand.
  • Lateral steps — work the legs in a direction most routines ignore.
  • Punches and arm swings — upper-body cardio with no impact on the joints.

A 15-minute no-equipment session

  1. Warm up, 3 min — Swim: long arm strokes.
  2. Legs, 5 min — Move Rush and Squat Rush, alternating rounds.
  3. Upper body, 5 min — Boxing and Slash.
  4. Cool down, 2 min — Penalty Hero at an easy pace, then stretch.

Low-impact options

If jumping is not for you, Slash, Boxing and Swim need no jumping at all, and Slash can be played sitting down.
